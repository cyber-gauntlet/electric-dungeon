# electric-dungeon
Cyber warfare in a neon electric dungeon

In this game, you traverse dungeons full of robotic spiders, drones that when they see you send out a signal that alerts swarms of robotic ant-like creatures where you are, and the occasional ED-209 like robots that will annihilate you.

You are a cyber-ninja with the ability to hack cyber-space. You're able to transport your mind into cyber-space, traverse the Neuro-nets to another location, and transfer into another body. Your skills will help you to save the city of New China, which sits above the cyber-gauntlet. The cyber-guantlet is an old-world structure of underground mazes that once fed New China with enormous amounts of data.

Test your cyber skills in the neon dungeons of cyber-guantlet.
